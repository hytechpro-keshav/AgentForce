# Agent 3 — The Knowledge Base Agent

_The expert who finds the right fix — from your own approved guidance._

Once a case is sorted and the customer story is clear, the next question is: **what should we actually do about this problem?** That's Agent 3. It searches your company's approved knowledge — troubleshooting guides, repair playbooks, safety policies, and service procedures — and brings back the best-matching guidance, with sources attached, so the rest of the team never has to guess or reinvent the answer.

---

## What it does (in one line)

**It finds approved, source-backed troubleshooting guidance for every case — tailored to the issue, the product, and the customer's situation — so your team acts on proven answers, not memory or guesswork.**

---

## Scope — what this agent owns (and what it does not)

**Agent 3 owns knowledge and guidance only.**

| In scope                                                                    | Out of scope                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Searching approved knowledge articles and playbooks                         | Scheduling a technician            |
| Matching guidance to the specific issue and product                         | Ordering or reserving parts        |
| Surfacing safety warnings and policy boundaries                             | Writing replies to the customer    |
| Recommending next actions (diagnostics, part replacement, escalation, etc.) | Changing anything in your systems  |
| Citing which articles and sources support the guidance                      | Making final approval decisions    |
| Flagging when no trusted guidance exists                                    | Promising outcomes to the customer |

Agent 3 **reads your knowledge base and summarizes what it finds**. It does not act, commit, or change records. It gives the rest of the team a trusted starting point for the fix.

---

## Topic — what this agent is about

**Approved troubleshooting guidance and policy-safe next steps.**

Every case arrives with a problem. Agent 2 tells you who the customer is. Agent 3 answers the questions your best technician would ask before touching the equipment:

- What does our official guidance say about this type of issue?
- Are there safety warnings we need to follow first?
- What diagnostic or repair steps are approved for this product?
- Does our knowledge suggest a part replacement, a site visit, or an escalation?
- Is there a policy boundary we need to respect (warranty, fee waiver, vendor handoff)?

Triage tells you _how urgent_ the case is. Customer history tells you _who you're dealing with_. Agent 3 tells you _what the approved fix looks like_.

---

## Why it matters

- **Every case gets a head start on the solution.** Your team doesn't start from a blank page — they start with your best documented guidance.
- **Answers come from your sources, not imagination.** Guidance is tied to real articles and playbooks your company has already approved.
- **Safety and policy are surfaced early.** Warnings about dangerous conditions, warranty limits, and escalation rules appear before anyone takes action.
- **Repeat issues get smarter answers.** Because Agent 3 knows the product model, warranty status, and prior fixes, it searches for guidance that fits _this_ situation — not a generic keyword match.
- **When nothing matches, it says so honestly.** If no trusted guidance exists, the team knows to escalate or investigate — instead of assuming an answer.
- **Every later step works from the same playbook.** Parts, scheduling, and final approval all see one consistent set of recommended actions.

---

## Steps it executes (the simple version)

Think of Agent 3 as a fast, careful librarian who only opens approved books:

1. **Receives the enriched case** — picks up where Agents 1 and 2 left off, with priority, customer context, and product details already in place.
2. **Frames the right question** — turns the case into a focused search using the issue description, urgency, product model, warranty status, and any repeat-issue signals.
3. **Searches approved knowledge** — looks across your trusted knowledge library for articles and playbooks that match the situation.
4. **Filters for quality and relevance** — ignores outdated content and low-confidence matches; only surfaces guidance that meets your trust bar.
5. **Assembles the guidance** — pulls together a clear summary of what to do, which sources support it, and how confident the match is.
6. **Highlights safety and policy** — flags warnings (for example, battery swelling, high-voltage risk, or warranty boundaries) so they are visible before action.
7. **Recommends next actions** — suggests typed next steps such as run a diagnostic, replace a part, schedule a visit, give the customer an instruction, or escalate to a vendor.
8. **Packages everything for the team** — delivers a structured guidance snapshot and passes the case to the next agent.

The whole process runs automatically in seconds — no one has to search multiple knowledge bases or ask a senior tech "have we seen this before?"

## Output — what Agent 3 produces

For every case, Agent 3 delivers a **Knowledge Guidance Package** — a ready-to-use snapshot the rest of the AI team (and your people) can rely on:

| Output                     | What it tells you                                                                                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Guidance status**        | Whether matching knowledge was found, nothing matched, or the search could not run.                           |
| **Plain-language summary** | A clear, readable explanation of what the approved guidance recommends.                                       |
| **Source citations**       | Which knowledge articles and playbooks the guidance came from — so anyone can verify it.                      |
| **Confidence level**       | How strong the match is, so the team knows when to trust the guidance and when to double-check.               |
| **Recommended actions**    | Specific next steps — diagnostics, part replacement, site visit, customer instructions, or vendor escalation. |
| **Suggested parts**        | When guidance points to a specific part, the safe part identifier and description.                            |
| **Safety flags**           | Warnings that must be seen before work begins — from informational notes to critical alerts.                  |
| **Policy boundaries**      | Signals when guidance touches warranty limits, fee waivers, or escalation rules.                              |

That's the fix playbook. Clear, sourced, and ready for the agents that check parts, schedule service, and run final approval.

## Built-in trust & safety

- **Approved sources only.** Agent 3 searches your vetted knowledge library — not the open internet and not improvised answers.
- **Honest when uncertain.** If no matching guidance exists, it reports that clearly instead of making something up.
- **Read-only.** It never changes knowledge articles, case records, or anything in your systems. It only reads and summarizes.
- **Privacy aware.** Searches use the issue and product context — not customer names, account numbers, or personal details.
- **Outdated content excluded.** Deprecated or end-of-life guidance is filtered out by default.
- **A specialist, not a decision-maker.** Agent 3 recommends what the knowledge says. It does not schedule, dispatch, order parts, or approve — those decisions belong to later agents and, where needed, to people.

## Where it fits in the bigger picture

Agent 3 is **step three of a connected AI service team.**

```
Agent 1 sorts & prioritizes  →  Agent 2 builds customer context  →  Agent 3 finds the fix  →  …
```

Once Agents 1 and 2 have prepared the case, Agent 3 enriches it with approved troubleshooting guidance and source-backed next steps. From there, specialized agents take over — checking parts and delivery, scheduling the right technician, and running a final human-approval check before anything is acted on.

In short: **Agent 3 turns your knowledge base into action — so every case is handled with proven guidance, not guesswork.**

# Agent 2 — The Customer History Agent

_The researcher who makes sure your team never starts blind._

After a case is sorted and prioritized, the next question is simple but critical: **who is this customer, and what do we already know about them?** That's Agent 2. It instantly pulls together everything your company already has on the customer — their history, their equipment, their service agreements, and any patterns — so every next step is informed, not guessed.

---

## What it does (in one line)

**It builds a complete picture of the customer behind every case — who they are, what they've experienced before, what equipment is involved, and how much is at stake — so the rest of the team acts with full context, not a blank slate.**

---

## Scope — what this agent owns (and what it does not)

**Agent 2 owns customer context only.**

| In scope                                          | Out of scope                            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Who the customer is (account, tier, relationship) | Scheduling a technician                 |
| Past service history and prior incidents          | Ordering or reserving parts             |
| Equipment and assets involved                     | Writing customer replies                |
| Warranty, contracts, and service-level agreements | Making promises to the customer         |
| Repeat-failure and escalation patterns            | Changing anything in your systems       |
| Business risk and account importance              | Approving actions (that's a later step) |

Agent 2 **reads and summarizes**. It does not act, commit, or change records. It gives the rest of the team the full story so they can act wisely.

---

## Topic — what this agent is about

**Customer context and history.**

Every case arrives with a problem description. Agent 2 answers the questions your best service rep would ask before picking up the phone:

- Who is this customer — and how important are they to the business?
- Have we seen this problem (or this equipment) before?
- Are they covered under warranty or a premium service agreement?
- Is this a one-off issue, or part of a recurring pattern?
- Is there an open renewal, escalation history, or other relationship signal we should know about?

Triage tells you _how urgent_ the case is. Agent 2 tells you _who you're dealing with_ and _what's really at stake_.

---

## Why it matters

- **No more "starting from zero."** Your team opens a case already knowing the customer's background.
- **Repeat problems get spotted instantly.** The same failure on the same equipment twice in a month doesn't look like a new ticket — it looks like what it is.
- **The right customers get the right treatment.** A critical issue from a strategic account with a premium SLA is handled differently than the same issue from a first-time reporter — and Agent 2 makes that difference visible immediately.
- **Customers aren't asked for information you already have.** Account details, warranty status, and past fixes are assembled automatically.
- **Every later agent works from the same facts.** Knowledge, parts, scheduling, and approvals all see one consistent customer picture — no conflicting stories.

---

## Steps it executes (the simple version)

Think of Agent 2 as a fast, thorough researcher who never forgets to check the file:

1. **Receives the sorted case** — picks up where Agent 1 left off, with priority and summary already in place.
2. **Identifies the customer** — pulls account profile, tier, segment, and key contacts from your records.
3. **Reviews service history** — scans prior cases, escalations, and how similar issues were resolved before.
4. **Checks equipment and assets** — finds what products or devices are involved, including model and deployment details.
5. **Verifies coverage** — looks up warranty status, contracts, entitlements, and service-level agreements.
6. **Spots patterns** — flags repeat failures, chronic issues, and concurrent open incidents.
7. **Assesses business importance** — weighs account tier, strategic value, renewal status, and overall risk.
8. **Packages everything for the team** — delivers a clear, structured customer snapshot and passes the case to the next agent.

The whole process runs automatically in seconds — no one has to search multiple screens or ask a colleague "do we know this account?"

---

## Output — what Agent 2 produces

For every case, Agent 2 delivers a **Customer Context Package** — a ready-to-use snapshot the rest of the AI team (and your people) can rely on:

| Output                       | What it tells you                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Customer tier**            | Premium, standard, or other segment — so tone and urgency match the relationship.                                |
| **Service level (SLA)**      | What response time or service standard the customer is entitled to under contract.                               |
| **Warranty status**          | Whether the involved equipment is covered, expired, or eligible for replacement.                                 |
| **Repeat-issue signal**      | Whether this same problem (or equipment) has happened before — and how often.                                    |
| **Escalation history**       | Prior escalations on this account, so nothing surprises the team.                                                |
| **Strategic account flag**   | Whether this is a key account that deserves extra care and visibility.                                           |
| **Equipment summary**        | What is installed, which models, and how many — the physical context of the issue.                               |
| **Open incident count**      | How many other active issues this customer already has open right now.                                           |
| **Business risk assessment** | A combined view of how serious this case is for the business — relationship, revenue, and pattern risk together. |

That's the full picture. Clear, structured, and ready for the agents that find the fix, check parts, schedule service, and run final approval.

---

## Built-in trust & safety

- **Facts over guesses.** Agent 2 only reports what it can find in your records. If something isn't documented, it says so — it never invents warranty coverage or strategic importance.
- **Read-only.** It never changes customer data, case records, or anything in your systems. It only reads and summarizes.
- **Privacy aware.** It works from business records, not raw personal details scattered in free text.
- **Transparent.** Every finding can be traced back to where it came from, so reviewers can verify the picture.
- **A specialist, not a decision-maker.** Agent 2 describes the situation. It does not schedule, dispatch, escalate, or promise — those decisions belong to later agents and, where needed, to people.

---

## Where it fits in the bigger picture

Agent 2 is **step two of a connected AI service team.**

```
Agent 1 sorts & prioritizes  →  Agent 2 builds customer context  →  Agent 3 finds the fix  →  …
```

Once Agent 1 has read, prioritized, and summarized a case, Agent 2 enriches it with everything your company already knows about that customer. From there, specialized agents take over — finding approved solutions, checking parts and delivery, scheduling the right technician, and running a final human-approval check before anything is acted on.

In short: **Agent 2 makes every case personal — so your team treats the customer, not just the ticket.**

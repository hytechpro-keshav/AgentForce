# Agent 1 — The Triage Agent

_The first responder of your AI service team._

When a customer issue comes in, someone has to read it, decide how urgent it is, and point it in the right direction — fast. That's Agent 1. It's the very first agent that looks at every new case, sizes it up in seconds, and sets the whole process in motion.

---

## What it does (in one line)

**It reads every incoming case the moment it arrives, decides how urgent it is, writes a short plain-English summary, and recommends the next step — instantly, around the clock.**

---

## Why it matters

- **No case waits in a queue to be sorted.** Every case is reviewed the second it lands.
- **Urgent issues get flagged first.** Critical problems jump the line instead of sitting unnoticed.
- **Your team starts with a head start.** Instead of a blank screen, agents open a case that's already summarized and prioritized.
- **It never sleeps.** Nights, weekends, holidays — triage happens 24/7.
- **It's consistent.** Every case is judged the same way, every time, with no "bad day" variability.

---

## What it actually decides

For every case, Agent 1 produces three simple things:

1. **A priority level** — Low, Normal, High, or Critical.
2. **A short summary** — a clean one-line description of what the issue is.
3. **A recommended next step** — what should happen next to move the case forward.

That's it. Clear, quick, and ready for the next agent (or a human) to act on.

---

## How it works (the simple version)

Think of it like a smart front desk that never takes a break:

1. **It reads the new case** — pulling in the details as soon as the case is created.
2. **It makes the call** — how urgent is this, what's it about, what should happen next.
3. **It passes it along** — handing the case, now sorted and summarized, to the rest of the team.

The whole thing happens in seconds, automatically, with no one needing to press a button.

---

## Built-in trust & safety

- **Privacy first.** Sensitive personal details are stripped out before anything is processed — names, emails, phone numbers, and account details are never exposed.
- **Always has a backup.** If anything is unclear, it safely defaults to the customer's stated urgency and routes the case to a human — it never guesses wildly.
- **Fully transparent.** Every decision is recorded, so you can always see _why_ a case was prioritized the way it was.
- **A team player, not a lone wolf.** Agent 1 only handles triage. The bigger decisions — and any approvals — are handled later by other specialized agents and, where needed, by people.

---

## Where it fits in the bigger picture

Agent 1 is **step one of a connected AI service team.** Once it sorts and summarizes a case, it hands off to the next specialists in line — agents that pull up customer history, find the right fix, check parts and scheduling, and run a final human-approval check before anything is acted on.

In short: **Agent 1 gets every case moving in the right direction, instantly — so nothing falls through the cracks and your people focus on solving, not sorting.**
